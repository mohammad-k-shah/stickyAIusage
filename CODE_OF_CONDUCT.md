# Code of conduct

Sticky AI Usage follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and
keep criticism about the work, never the person. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing someone's
private details are not accepted anywhere in this project: issues, pull requests, Discussions and reviews.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here worries you, tell the maintainer privately through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/mohammad-k-shah/stickyAIusage/security/advisories/new) (mark it "Code of
conduct"), or by a direct message to [@mohammad-k-shah](https://github.com/mohammad-k-shah) on GitHub. Reports are read
in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and warn, or block someone from the project, as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
